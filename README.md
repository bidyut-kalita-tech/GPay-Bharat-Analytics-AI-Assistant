# 💳 Google Pay Bharat Analytics Dashboard & Generative AI Assistant

An end-to-end FinTech analytics and conversational business intelligence project analyzing over **250,000+ Google Pay UPI transactions (₹327.94M volume)** across India.

## 🚀 Key Highlights & Business Insights

* **Platform Performance:** Analyzed 250,000 transactions with an overall **95.05% success rate** and **₹327.94M** gross volume.
* **Temporal Patterns:** **71.5%** of transactions occur on **Weekdays**, showing high reliance on daily utility and transit payments.
* **Top Spending Categories:** **Shopping & Grocery** capture the largest share of transactional volume.
* **Core Demographic:** The **26–35 age cohort** is the dominant user segment driving **34.97%** of transactions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Custom UI/UX
* **Data Science & Modeling:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook (EDA & Hypothesis Testing)
* **Generative AI & LLM:** Google Gemini API (`gemini-1.5-flash`), Prompt Engineering (Text-to-SQL)
* **Web App & Backend:** Streamlit, SQLAlchemy, PyMySQL, MySQL Database
